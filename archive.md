# Archive — History of decisions

> Receives whatever leaves a `memory.md` because it was replaced or superseded: changed decisions, closed goals, states that no longer hold. Entries are added on top; the past is never rewritten.

Entry format:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — [short title of the event]

[What held before, what holds now, why it changed. The old content is copied here in full before being overwritten in memory.md.]
```
